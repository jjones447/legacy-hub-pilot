Crisis & urgent help

Crisis Help

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988 — free, confidential support 24/7 for anyone in emotional distress, including overwhelmed caregivers.

Crisis Help

Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline

Call 1-800-272-3900 any time, day or night — trained staff answer questions about dementia, caregiving, and local support.

alz.org/help-support →
Crisis Help

IMPACT 211

Dial 211 for connections to help of every kind across Greater Milwaukee — food, housing, care services, and caregiver support.

Medical emergency

Dementia & aging

Dementia-specific organizations

Education

Alzheimer's Association — Wisconsin Chapter

Education programs, support groups, care consultations, and the 24/7 helpline — the largest dementia-focused nonprofit in the state.

alz.org/wi →
Support Groups

Aging & Disability Resource Center (ADRC) of Milwaukee County

The county's front door for aging services — benefits counseling, caregiver support, respite options, and long-term care planning.

county.milwaukee.gov →
Education

Wisconsin Alzheimer's Institute

University of Wisconsin program advancing dementia care — memory clinics and resources for families across the state.

wai.wisc.edu →

Dementia-friendly community programs

Many Wisconsin communities run dementia-friendly initiatives — memory cafés, trained businesses, and inclusive events. Ask us which are active near you.
