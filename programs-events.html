Drop-In Respite

Sometimes, you just need a few hours to yourself.

Our Drop-In Respite program offers family caregivers a chance to take a break while their loved ones living with dementia spend time in a welcoming, supportive environment. Through engaging activities and compassionate, non-medical supervision, we provide peace of mind while you rest, run errands, attend appointments, or simply take time for yourself.

Event Respite Services

We partner with organizations to bring respite services directly to community events, making it easier for family caregivers to attend and participate.

Whether you’re hosting a conference, workshop, or community gathering, our team can help create a supportive space where loved ones living with dementia can enjoy meaningful activities while their caregivers participate in the event.

Family Education & Workshops

Caring for a loved one with dementia comes with questions, challenges, and moments when you’re just not sure what to do next.

Our educational programs provide practical information, helpful strategies, and opportunities to better understand dementia and the caregiving experience.

Our educational offerings include:

  • Care at Home with Lauryn
    Practical tips and everyday strategies to help families navigate caring for a loved one with dementia at home.
  • Dementia Live
    An interactive, hands-on experience that helps participants better understand some of the challenges people living with dementia may face, encouraging greater patience, empathy, and understanding.

We also offer workshops throughout the year that connect caregivers with professionals, resources, and information to support them along the way.
