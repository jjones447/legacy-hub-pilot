Caregiver Sanctuary Community Series

Connection. Community. Time for You.

View Upcoming Events Partner With Us

Caregiving can be isolating. Between the responsibilities, routines, and demands of caring for someone else, it can be easy to lose opportunities to connect with others or simply enjoy time for yourself.

Our Caregiver Sanctuary Community Series was created to change that.

Through gatherings and experiences hosted in partnership with local businesses and community spaces, we bring family caregivers together to connect, build relationships, and enjoy experiences that support their well-being.

Created with dementia family caregivers in mind, the Community Series offers a welcoming space to meet others who understand the caregiving journey while enjoying moments that aren’t centered on caregiving itself.

What to Expect

From conversations over coffee to creative activities and wellness experiences, our gatherings offer opportunities to step outside your everyday routine, meet new people, and enjoy time that’s centered on you.

Whether you’re joining us for the first time or returning to connect with familiar faces, there’s a place for you here.
