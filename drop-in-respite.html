Drop-In Respite Program

A Break for You. Meaningful Moments for Your Loved One.

How it works

Everyone deserves an opportunity to take a break, especially those caring for someone living with dementia.

Our free Drop-In Respite program offers caregivers time for themselves while their loved ones enjoy meaningful activities, social connection, and compassionate support in a welcoming environment.

Whether you need to run errands, attend appointments, or simply recharge, we're here to help.

What Makes Our Respite Different?

We believe respite should be more than supervision. It should be a meaningful experience for the person living with dementia, too.

Our program uses a person-centered approach, recognizing each individual's unique interests, abilities, preferences, and life experiences.

🎵

Meaningful Engagement

Music, creative activities, games, conversation, and opportunities for social connection.

🧡

Person-Centered Care

Activities and interactions adapted to each participant's interests, strengths, and abilities.

🤝

Compassionate Support

Dementia-trained team members providing supervision and non-medical support in a welcoming environment.

☕

Time for Caregivers

An opportunity to rest, attend appointments, run errands, or focus on personal well-being.

How It Works

  1. 1

    Register

    Select an upcoming respite date and complete the registration form.

  2. 2

    Connect With Our Team

    For first-time participants, our team will follow up to learn more about your loved one's needs, preferences, and how we can best support them.

  3. 3

    Take Some Time for Yourself

    Once participation is confirmed, your loved one can enjoy meaningful activities and companionship while you take time for yourself.

Bring Respite to Your Faith Community

Faith communities have long been places of connection, compassion, and care. We believe they can also play an important role in supporting families navigating dementia.

Legacy partners with faith communities to expand access to respite through caregiver education, dementia training, and collaborative program development.

Together, we can create welcoming spaces where caregivers receive support and individuals living with dementia experience meaningful engagement.
