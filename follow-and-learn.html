Good information is hard to find and easy to fake. These are people whose work we think is worth your time.

Voices worth following

Dementia and caregiving accounts, online educators and people whose work we think is worth your time.

Adria Thompson | Be Light Care

Speech-language pathologist and dementia educator sharing practical, easy-to-understand strategies for communication, behaviors, personal care, and everyday dementia caregiving.

Instagram · Website

Dr. Macie P. Smith | Just Ask Dr. Macie

Gerontologist and licensed social worker sharing approachable dementia education to help families better understand behaviors, communication, caregiving, and aging.

Instagram · Links

Jessica C. Guthrie | When Career & Caregiving Collide

Family caregiver, advocate, educator, and speaker sharing an honest look at caring for her mother living with Alzheimer's disease and navigating caregiving as a young Black caregiver.

Instagram · Website · Substack

Carrie Aalberts | Dementia Darling

Gerontologist and dementia educator sharing caregiver education, resources, encouragement, and a more joyful, human-centered perspective on dementia and aging.

Instagram · Website

Dr. Natali Edmonds | Dementia Careblazers

Board-certified geropsychologist providing practical dementia education to help family caregivers navigate behaviors, safety, caregiver stress, and everyday caregiving challenges.

Instagram · YouTube · Website

Teepa Snow | Positive Approach to Care

Dementia care educator and occupational therapist known for practical, hands-on approaches to understanding brain changes, communication, behaviors, and supporting people living with dementia.

Instagram · YouTube · Website

Jacquelyn Revere | Mom of My Mom

Former millennial family caregiver who shares her experience caring for her mother with dementia, including the realities of caregiving, grief, identity, advocacy, and life after caregiving.

Instagram · Website

Ashley Stevens | The Dementia Guru

Gerontologist and licensed clinical social worker sharing dementia education with a focus on caregiving challenges, family dynamics, care decisions, and supporting families throughout the dementia journey.

Instagram · Website

Rev. Katie Norris | Creative Connections Dementia

Dementia educator sharing relationship-centered and trauma-informed approaches to dementia care, with an emphasis on connection, communication, and helping families create meaningful experiences.

Instagram · Website

Dementia Success Path

Practical dementia caregiving education focused on challenging behaviors, activities, caregiver self-care, and strategies families can use in everyday life.

Instagram · Website
