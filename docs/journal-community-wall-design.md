## A. Guided journal: caregiver controls

Proposed portal entry: My Wellness → Journal, explicitly private by default. Start with a small reviewed prompt catalog and optional free writing, not ongoing chat. Draft prompt examples for later content approval: “What felt manageable today?” and “What would you like to make room for tomorrow?” Skip prompts/AI/check-ins freely; no coercive completion score. A journal entry is not therapy, counseling, diagnosis or emergency support.

Proposed states: private entry → explicitly shared entry → revoked/private; owner can delete from either state. No automatic share, automatic reflection or reuse for Staff notes/model training/other caregivers. Each entry's share confirmation names recipient scope and exact revision/text being shared. A concurrent edit invalidates that confirmation; no permission silently extends to later revisions. Owner may revise privately or explicitly share the revised snapshot. Proposed bounded entry size8KiB UTF-8, list pages20, no attachments initially, all subject to review. Do not accept a client owner ID/email, unlimited body, arbitrary provider URL or shared-by boolean as authority.

Proposed per-entry association: immutable opaque entry ID + verified caregiver ID, revision, encrypted payload reference, timestamps; sharing grant separately references entry+revision, audience permission, consent time and revocation time. No SQL/schema number reserved. Staff shared reads must join a CURRENT unrevoked grant to CURRENT active Staff permission, not read the journal table directly. No unshared text in search, list previews, notifications, generic audit logs, traces, error dumps or AI prompts. Proposed audit keeps operation/opaque ID/outcome only; even those metadata reveal use and need bounded permission/retention.

Per-entry revoke denies subsequent Staff reads immediately and invalidates caches/exports still under system control. Staff may already have read/copied/screenshot text; revocation cannot erase human memory or uncontrolled copies. Avoid bulk shared exports in phase1; any future export has explicit policy, consent purpose and revocation/retention implications. Do not market a technical inability to retain previously viewed material.

## Journal privacy architecture choices — decision required

| Option | Protection and access | Tradeoffs / missing decisions |
|---|---|---|
| Server-managed encryption at rest with scoped keys | Keeps ordinary Staff API/UI out of private content; storage theft risk reduced if keys are separate | Application/operator/key custodians may decrypt. “Legacy cannot access private text” is NOT satisfied against operators. Key management, least privilege, rotation/recovery and backup access need proof. |
| Client-side encryption with caregiver-held key | Server/backup hold ciphertext; can prevent routine server/operator decryption if correctly implemented | Lost key may mean lost entries. Recovery escrow can reintroduce operator access. Multi-device unlock, malicious delivered client code, metadata leakage and accessibility need review. No zero-access guarantee from choosing a label. |
| No stored journal text (local session only) | Avoids server content storage; optional content-free activity receipt | Not durable across devices; browser persistence/export can create different risks. Does not satisfy durable journal expectations without explicit customer acceptance. |

Recommendation for review, not a selected architecture: do not launch durable private text until qualified owner/Jacob chooses and verifies a model that meets the stated threat model. If “Legacy should not have access” includes operators, plain D1 text or server-held decrypt keys alone are insufficient. Staff UI restrictions and encryption are separate controls. Under client encryption, deliberate per-entry sharing may create a separately encrypted shared snapshot for authorized Staff; revoke can remove keys under our control but cannot retract downloaded plaintext. Do not silently downgrade encryption to make AI/recovery simpler. Backups must preserve encryption without leaking keys beside ciphertext; test lost/rotated keys and recovery before promising restorability.

## Optional per-use AI reflection

Default AI OFF, no provider request on save, load, typing, prompt selection or Passport recording. Before EACH use, show the exact entry revision selected, that text goes to the named external AI provider, what data leaves, allowed purpose and verified retention/training settings. A separate affirmative action authorizes ONE bounded reflection; ordinary save/share consent does not. Cancel or declining AI still permits journaling. Provider unavailable/refused/unknown outcome must not prevent saving or suggest completion. Never automatically replay a timed-out request, silently fall back to another provider, stream private content into telemetry or start a continuing chat.

Provider/account/domain selection, no-training/no-retention capability and actual configured agreement/settings require Jacob and qualified review. If those cannot be verified, AI stays unavailable; no vendor/no-retention promise is invented. Decide whether a reflection is stored, encrypted with the entry, or discarded (proposed default: discard unless owner explicitly saves it). Keep costs/rate bounds and error handling content-free. Provider logs/retries/abuse retention may limit deletion; disclose real terms rather than claiming a deletion button purges provider systems. Client-held encryption necessarily exposes selected plaintext on the owner's device/provider during authorized use; it does not make AI processing end-to-end private from that provider.

Safety is a qualified content policy, not a clinical service: supportive reflection must not diagnose, prescribe, impersonate a counselor or provide emergency response. An always-visible route to the existing crisis-help resources is independent of detection. If chosen-use or deliberately shared text triggers a reviewed safety rule, show the existing U.S.988/911 resource choices and do not attempt crisis counseling. Private entries must not be scanned server-side or sent to Staff merely for detection: that would contradict privacy/default consent. Any on-device detection has false negatives/positives and cannot promise protection; model-based detection itself needs provider consent. No automated emergency-service contact. Recipient awareness/queueing and human response hours must be agreed and disclosed; “alert” does not mean monitored or answered.

## Journal deletion, backup/restore, usage and wellness

Owner deletion removes active content, shared snapshots/keys under our control, reflection copies, grants and derived snippets in an atomic or durably reconciled workflow. Cancel active share/provider work and fence late writes by revision/tombstone; an in-flight provider response must not resurrect a deleted entry. Historical audit, provider-held material and backup ciphertext need separate policy/disclosure. Adopt a reviewed removal ledger or equally strong restore reconciliation before launch; restore old backup into quarantine, replay deletions/revocations/key retirements, then reauthorize exposure. Ledger identity/access/retention and eventual ciphertext expiry are unresolved operating decisions. No invented30-day journal retention or claim all backups are erased immediately.

Optional “Record that I journaled” sends only an opaque idempotency key, activity type journaled and date/time (and agreed identity association), never prompt/text/reflection/tags/word count. Decide permitted granularity, one-per-day deduplication and milestone rules in existing Passport work; do not equate a click with verified wellbeing or grant qualification. Proposed repeated identical key returns one original result; changed payload conflicts. Private entry deletion and usage-event deletion are distinct choices, clearly explained; default behavior and withdrawal/correction/export need approved policy. Do not make journal text preservation a prerequisite for a content-free activity.

Legacy's usage view is counts/dates under agreed role permissions; aggregate small groups can identify people, so suppress small slices or restrict views after policy review. Optional existing wellness check-in is separate, explicitly consented, can be skipped and carries existing visibility rules; do not copy journal text into its optional note or change questionnaire/scoring/schedule under this task. Descriptive trends are not evidence the journal caused improvements. Source/public wording about tracking participation does not authorize unrestricted new surveillance.

## B. Members-only community wall

Decided product scope: one wall, signed-in members only, posts appear immediately without preapproval, Staff may delete. Shanelle is the initial configurable report/crisis recipient. This is a community space, not a confidential journal; clearly explain other eligible members can read/copy posts. Never reuse journal content, AI reflections, email address or profile details as a post. No automatic sharing or search-engine indexing.

Eligibility must be checked on EACH read/write against current verified session/nonarchived caregiver AND actual Sanctuary membership; ordinary portal access alone is not sufficient. Qualify the real stored membership predicate/value, account suspension/revocation and Staff moderator permission before implementation. Client role flags cannot grant membership/moderation. Same-origin/CSRF, strict bounded methods/body, no cross-owner writes, no public API/feed/cache/search preview. Responses private/no-store with noindex headers, but robots headers are not access control. Avoid third-party analytics/embedded trackers, public media attachments or unfurling links. Phase1 proposal: text-only/plain escaped output, no HTML/attachments/DMs/comments/reactions until separately scoped.

Proposed privacy-preserving display: caregiver-approved first name/initial or chosen community display name; never email, caregiver ID, loved-one name, health condition or address by default. Warn that a display name is not anonymity and content can identify people. Decide collision/impersonation handling and edits to display name; bind server-side owner privately. Never trust a submitted author label or expose owner ID through URLs. No fabricated display identity/real-user fixtures.

Proposed post max2000 characters/8KiB body, feed20 with bounded cursor, per-member5posts/hour and1report/post/member; numbers are review proposals, not applied policy. Enforce server-side atomic abuse quotas and idempotency scoped to owner/action; concurrent retries cannot double-post or overwhelm alerts. Block unbounded query/filter/export endpoints. Guidelines on first post and accessible thereafter: avoid identifying another person or sharing private health/contact details, be respectful, no medical/legal instructions or solicitation; a saved guideline acknowledgment is not permission to expose someone's data. Immediate posting means unsuitable material may be visible BEFORE moderation; requester accepted no preapproval, but operating owner must accept staffing/risk controls before launch.

## Wall report, hide, delete and queue contract

- Report: authenticated eligible member chooses bounded reason, optional bounded note, receives generic receipt. Reporter identity/reason not visible to other members or reported author; Staff sees only needed queue projection. Don't allow reports to disclose existence of inaccessible/deleted posts. Proposed report immediately hides that post FROM THAT REPORTER; global auto-hide based on counts is not approved and vulnerable to brigading.
- Hide: member may hide a post for their own view without reporting. Owner-scoped preference, not a claim the content is deleted for everyone; avoid browser-only persistence if shared-device privacy is required. Storage/lifecycle policy needed.
- Own delete: current owner may remove their post; repeated delete is idempotent. No foreign owner IDs or URL guessing. Proposed phase1 no post edit to avoid unseen text changes after a moderation decision; future editing requires revision invalidation/review semantics.
- Staff deletion: current authorized moderator can remove any post with bounded reason and audit. Transaction/CAS protects races with own deletion/reports; post disappears from feeds/caches promptly. Do not retain removed text in generic logs. If an abuse-evidence hold is necessary, define purpose/access/expiry separately and disclose it; none is silently invented here.
- Report/moderation queue is durable and works WITHOUT email or AI. Proposed states new/acknowledged/resolved, revision+claim lease for concurrent moderators, explicit disposition/outcome/audit. Shanelle's identity must be qualified as current authorized Staff/moderator before access; a named recipient is not a credential or permission grant. Configurable later through the governed authorization path.

Safety resources always available; reviewed wall-text detection may show resources to author/viewer and create a bounded Staff safety queue item, but detection is imperfect and may miss crises or flag noncrises. Do not promise round-the-clock review, emergency intervention or automated911 contact. Choose disclosed response expectations, escalation owner/backup, queue triage and absence handling BEFORE launch. Minimal notification contains an opaque authorized Staff link, not post/journal content or reporter details. Email is separately gated by existing provider/sending authority; outbox idempotency/retry/unknown-outcome handling and recipient verification required. Failed/unknown email must not lose the durable queue item or report; no blind resend/acceptance-equals-delivery claim.

Wall deletion/export/backup/restore needs its own active-removal vs audit/evidence retention policy, not automatic reuse of journal-private semantics. Restore must reapply member blocks, hidden/deleted posts and moderation decisions before serving. Owner export must not leak other members/reporters; no wall bulk export by default. Operator/backup access remains separate from member/Staff UI controls. Moderation needs access to member-visible content; that does not permit private-journal access.

## Proposed acceptance cases (NOT executed features)

1. Private journal read/list/write denied to foreign owner, ordinary Staff, stale/revoked/archived session; duplicate cookie/off-origin/missing CSRF/oversize/unknown fields fail closed without content logging. No text visible through usage/export/search/error paths.
2. Save/AI-off/decline reflection sends ZERO provider requests. Per-use consent pins exact revision/provider; edit races refuse. Timeout remains unknown, no alternate provider/replay; provider outcome/content not logged. No therapy/emergency behavior.
3. Share only chosen revision to authorized Staff; foreign/draft/revoked grants refuse. Revocation cancels delayed reads; delete cancels/fences late AI/share writes. Explain already-viewed copies truthfully.
4. Key-loss/rotation/recovery and backup theft simulations match chosen threat model; restore old backup cannot resurrect deleted text/shares/usage preferences. Test active-delete vs ledger retention and approved owner export without real private entries.
5. Journaled receipt contains no content and retries count once; skip check-in/AI/share still records voluntary activity. Check-in notes never autofill from journal; no causal outcome/grant claim.
6. Wall nonmember/anonymous/revoked read and cross-owner delete denied; initial membership signup does not bypass verified sign-in. No public feed/index/cache. Stored XSS/link/author-spoof fixtures render inert.
7. Immediate posts visible only to eligible members; report hides only for reporter, duplicates do not spam queue, member hides aren't global. Staff delete/own delete race resolves once; removed post not resurrected by pagination/restore.
8. Moderator revocation during queue read/write refuses; exact moderator identity/role needed. No journal text can enter wall/Staff by implicit association. Queue/report remains available on sender failure; notification unknown outcome doesn't double-send.
9. Resources accessible without detection; synthetic safety examples show resource/queue behavior only where consent/visibility permits. False-negative/false-positive limits explicit; no automatic emergency contact/continuous-monitoring guarantee.
10. All fixtures fictional and inert. These scenarios require implementation plus actual supported D1/key/provider/runtime qualification later; a design checklist is not test PASS.
