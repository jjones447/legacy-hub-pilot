CAREGIVER SANCTUARY MEMBERSHIP

More than a membership. A community that feels like family.

Caring for someone living with dementia comes with a lot of questions, challenges, and moments when you just need someone who understands.

That's why we created the Caregiver Sanctuary.

Our free membership gives you one place to connect with Legacy, find support, access helpful resources, and stay informed about programs and opportunities available to you.

Joining is free. You'll create a personal profile where you can stay connected with Legacy, access resources, register for events, and request support.

More than support. A sense of family.

At Legacy, we believe support should feel personal.

We're not just here to offer programs and resources. We're here to build real relationships with the caregivers we serve. We get to know you, your loved one, and the challenges you're navigating because we know that no two caregiving journeys look the same.

Over time, many of the caregivers we support become like family. We celebrate together, check in on one another, share difficult moments, and remind each other that we don't have to do this alone.

That's what the Caregiver Sanctuary is all about. A place where you're known, supported, and genuinely cared for.

What You'll Find Inside the Sanctuary

Whether you're looking for answers, a little breathing room, or simply someone who understands, we're here to help you find the support that makes sense for you.

◇

A Community That Understands

Connect with other caregivers through our weekly virtual support group and community gatherings. Share experiences, ask questions, or simply listen. There's no pressure to have everything figured out.

◇

Help When You Need It

Learn about respite opportunities, caregiver support, and ways our team can help you navigate challenges at home. We're here to listen and help you explore your options.

◇

Events and Opportunities

Stay connected to caregiver workshops, wellness activities, educational sessions, and special events designed with caregivers in mind.

◇

Resources in One Place

Find practical information, educational materials, and helpful tools to support you throughout your caregiving journey.

Opportunities depend on program availability and caregiver needs.

Support That Grows With You

Every caregiving journey is different, and what you need today may not be what you need six months from now. Your Caregiver Sanctuary portal gives you a personal space to stay connected with Legacy, explore resources, register for events, and request support as your needs change. It's one more way for us to stay connected and make sure you know what's available when you need it.

  • View upcoming events and your registrations
  • Request caregiver support
  • Explore available respite opportunities
  • Access caregiver resources
  • Discover wellness activities and tools
  • Stay connected with the Legacy team

There's a Place for You Here.

Whether you're new to caregiving, have been doing this for years, or are simply trying to figure out what comes next, you are welcome here.

You don't need to have all the answers. You don't even need to know exactly what kind of help you need.

Just know that you don't have to navigate it all alone.

Membership is always free.
