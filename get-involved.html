Get Involved

Together, we're building a community that cares for caregivers.

Supporting families affected by dementia takes more than one organization. It takes individuals, businesses, and community partners who believe caregivers deserve access to rest, wellness, education, and meaningful connection.

Whether you're looking to volunteer, partner, sponsor, donate, or share your expertise, there are meaningful ways to make a difference with Legacy.

Inquiry buttons open Legacy’s existing contact information. The partnership inquiry form is being prepared separately.

Partner With Legacy

Meaningful partnerships. Stronger communities.

Become a Caregiver Sanctuary Partner

Help create dementia-informed, caregiver-friendly spaces through community awareness, sharing resources, or displaying the Caregiver Sanctuary decal. Partners can also take Legacy's training.

Become a Caregiver Sanctuary Partner

Community & Corporate Partnerships

Bringing caregiver support into the places people live, work, and connect.

Legacy works with employers, healthcare organizations, churches, and community partners to make caregiver support more accessible.

Through facilitated support groups, caregiver wellness sessions, and educational experiences, we can bring meaningful support directly to the people your organization serves.

Whether you're interested in hosting programming, supporting an ongoing caregiver group, or exploring a funded partnership, we'd love to connect.
