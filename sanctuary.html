The idea

What is the Caregiver Sanctuary Network?

The Caregiver Sanctuary Network is Legacy's approach to caregiver support: by partnering with local businesses, organizations, and community spaces, we're creating welcoming environments where caregivers can access support, build relationships, prioritize their well-being, and find trusted resources throughout their community.
