The idea

What is Caregiver Sanctuary?

Caregiver Sanctuary is Legacy's approach to caregiver support: by partnering with local businesses, organizations, and community spaces, we're creating welcoming environments where caregivers can access support, build relationships, prioritize their well-being, and find trusted resources throughout their community.

Join free as a caregiver member, then find support wherever you see Caregiver Sanctuary — programs, partner locations, and community events.

OUR NETWORK

Caregiver Sanctuary Network

Bringing caregiver support into the community.

Caregiver support shouldn't be limited to a single building, program, or service. We believe caregivers deserve to feel welcomed, understood, and supported wherever life takes them.

The Caregiver Sanctuary Network is Legacy's growing network of local businesses, organizations, and community spaces committed to creating more caregiver-friendly environments.

Through dementia education, community partnerships, and wellness opportunities, we're helping everyday spaces become places where caregivers can connect, prioritize their well-being, and feel supported beyond their caregiving responsibilities.

Every Caregiver Sanctuary Partner completes Legacy's caregiver-friendly and dementia-informed training, equipping them with practical tools to better understand and support family caregivers and individuals living with dementia.

Together, we're making caregiver support a natural part of community life.

Building communities that understand and support caregivers.

PARTNER WITH US

Become a Caregiver Sanctuary Partner

Help us build a community that cares for caregivers.

Supporting caregivers is a community effort, and every business or organization has an opportunity to make a meaningful difference.

By joining the Caregiver Sanctuary Network, your business or organization becomes part of a growing community committed to making caregiver support more accessible, inclusive, and part of everyday life.

As a Caregiver Sanctuary Partner, you'll:

  • Build Understanding: Complete Legacy's caregiver-friendly and dementia-informed training to better understand and respond to the needs of caregivers and individuals living with dementia.
  • Create Welcoming Spaces: Help foster environments where caregivers and their loved ones feel respected, included, and supported.
  • Support Caregiver Wellness: Contribute to opportunities for caregivers to prioritize their well-being, connect with others, and engage in meaningful community experiences.
  • Make a Community Impact: Join a growing network working to reduce caregiver isolation and strengthen support for families affected by dementia.

Whether you're a coffee shop, wellness provider, community organization, or local business, there's an opportunity to make a difference.

Together, we can build communities where caregivers feel seen, supported, and valued.
