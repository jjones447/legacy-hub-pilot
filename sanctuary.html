Our Approach

What Is Caregiver Sanctuary?

Caring for a loved one with dementia can bring a lot of changes, questions, and responsibilities. And somewhere in the middle of all that, it’s easy to forget about yourself.

Caregiver Sanctuary was created with caregivers in mind.

We bring together respite, education, wellness opportunities, and people who understand what you’re experiencing. Sometimes that means having a few hours to yourself. Other times, it’s getting answers to a question, connecting with another caregiver, or doing something you enjoy.

Whatever brings you here, we want you to feel welcome and know you don’t have to figure everything out alone.

OUR NETWORK

Caregiver Sanctuary Network

Bringing Caregiver Support Into Our Community

Caregivers are part of every community, but that doesn’t always mean they know where to find support.

We’re working with local businesses, churches, wellness providers, and community organizations to help change that.

Some partners help by sharing resources or displaying a Caregiver Sanctuary decal. Others may host activities, offer wellness experiences, or participate in dementia education.

There isn’t just one way to support caregivers. What matters is creating more opportunities for families to feel welcome and connected.

Caregiver-Friendly & Dementia-Informed Training

Helping Our Community Better Understand Dementia

Caring for someone with dementia can come with challenges that aren’t always visible to others.

Our training helps businesses and organizations better understand dementia, recognize what caregivers may be experiencing, and learn ways to make their spaces more welcoming to families.

Training is available to interested organizations, but it is not required for every Caregiver Sanctuary Partner.
