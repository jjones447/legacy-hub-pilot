Supporting Dementia Caregivers Through Community, Wellness, and Respite.

At Legacy Home and Respite Care Foundation, we believe caregivers deserve support, too. Through respite, wellness programming, education, and meaningful community connections, we help family caregivers navigate the dementia journey without feeling alone.

Find Support Donate
You are not alone

Caring for someone living with dementia is one of life's most challenging journeys.

Whether you've recently received a diagnosis or have been caregiving for years, you don't have to navigate it alone. Legacy Home and Respite Care Foundation exists to create spaces where caregivers feel supported, connected, and empowered. We believe that when caregivers are cared for, families are stronger, communities are healthier, and individuals living with dementia experience better quality of life.

How we help

Supporting caregivers in every stage of the journey.

🤝

Community

Build meaningful connections with other caregivers who understand your experience.

💚

Wellness

Prioritize your own health through wellness programming, self-care opportunities, and caregiver-centered experiences.

📚

Education

Gain practical knowledge, tools, and confidence through workshops, support groups, and educational resources.

🏡

Respite

Take time to recharge while your loved one participates in engaging, supervised, non-medical activities.

🧭

Resources

Access trusted information, community services, and caregiver support tailored to your family's needs.

🌐

Community Partnerships

Connecting caregivers to dementia-friendly businesses, organizations, and experiences throughout the community.

Caregiver Sanctuary Network

Building communities where caregivers don't have to navigate dementia alone.

The Caregiver Sanctuary Network is Legacy's innovative approach to caregiver support. By partnering with local businesses, organizations, and community spaces, we're creating welcoming environments where caregivers can access support, build relationships, prioritize their well-being, and find trusted resources throughout their community.
